# Support

## Product support

Youniverse is in private beta. Beta participants should use the contact channel
provided with their invitation for account and product support.

## Safety

YouthMind is not an emergency service. If someone may be in immediate danger,
contact local emergency services or an appropriate crisis service in their region.

## Bugs and feature requests

Use the relevant repository's issue tracker when available. Never include
passwords, verification codes, access tokens, private wellbeing content, or
other personal information in an issue.
